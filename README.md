# What even are growth rates?

If you are not familiar, Fire Emblem is a series of turn-based combat games from Nintendo. A quirk of the game is what's called "growth rates."
Essentially, each encounter your units face gives them a certain level of Experience Points (XP). When a character gains enough XP to cross the threshold to "level up,"
that is when their growth rates come into play. A character's growth rates are the percentage chance that the associated stat will increase by a single point upon level-up.

For example, the character Roy has a 60 growth in the Speed stat. This means that, on average, his Speed should increase by 1 point on 60% of his level-ups. He starts at Level 1 and with a Speed stat of 7;
meaning that you can expect him to have a Speed stat of around 13 by Level 11.

Each game handles the calculations differently, but they all (so far) have included this feature.

# Why should I care what a character's growth rates are?

Do you need any of what I'm doing here to enjoy the Fire Emblem games? Not at all. Is it massively overkill for me to work on this? Certainly. But I think it's a lot of fun.

The point of this project is to provide a tool to assist the Fire Emblem enjoyer in determining which units to invest time and XP into. XP is a finite resource in these games,
and so there's nothing worse than a character getting to a high level but still having junk stats. These tools should be able to help avoid that for the most part; however bad luck can still tank even units with very high or balanced growth rates.
That's just the fun of the game :)
